    Type

    Making every sale count

    Inter for everything. Bold for headlines, regular for text. Regular case, no full stop at the end of a headline

    Always

    • Mist or white background, indigo text
    • Two colours on every post: indigo text next to a coral shape
    • Logo top left. Text and pattern never overlap
    • One pattern per post
    • Words your customers use: shop, sale, payout, refund

    Never

    • Black backgrounds, glow or rainbow gradients
    • Coral, teal or amber as text colour on light grounds
    • Small uppercase labels above headlines
    • Long dashes. Use a comma or a new sentence
    • Buzzwords: synergy, leverage, disrupt
