Cage-free apple farm declared “a huge success”

Apples are now allowed to roam freely

DRAW:a little family of apples running through a field. tiny legs.one rolling away. one baby apple staring straight at the reader.farmer proudly pointing.

A rural farm has become the first known agricultural facility to successfully release its apples from captivity.

Farm owner Sonya said the apples appear noticeably happier now that they can move freely through the grass.

“Look at that,” she said, pointing toward an apple rolling approximately three metres downhill. “They're free.”

The farm reported three apple babies were born this week. Witnesses say the newborns immediately began moving.

Officials are calling the development “encouraging,” although nobody has yet determined whether apples technically give birth.

The farm plans to expand its free-range programme next spring.
